Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who uses the app

The Pulau Terdampar app is used only by the creator of the series, with the creator's own accounts. It does not collect data from viewers or from other users.

2. Data the app uses

  • Access tokens and account IDs given by TikTok, Meta and Google when the creator logs in.
  • Basic profile information needed to post, such as the account name.
  • The video files, titles and captions that the creator chooses to publish.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish the creator's videos to the creator's own accounts, and to check that an upload finished.

4. Where the data is stored

Tokens and account IDs are stored only on the creator's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than the platform they belong to.

5. Sharing

The app does not sell or share data with anyone.

6. Deleting data

The creator can remove the app's access at any time in the settings of TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Google. Deleting the local tokens removes all stored data.
